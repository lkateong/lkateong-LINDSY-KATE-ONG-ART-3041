Campaign identity

Make Room to Relax — an unofficial student campaign celebrating Rilakkuma through comfort, playful everyday rituals, and tactile merchandise.

Palette

Warm ivory #FFF9EF · Oatmeal #EADCC8 · Cocoa #79573E · Milk brown #B88D68 · Blush #F5C8C8 · Butter yellow #F7D96B · Muted sage #B7C7A8.

Photography

Diffused daylight, delicate shadows, recognizable character shapes, authentic fabric textures, cozy editorial compositions, 15–30% breathing room for copy.

On brand

Warm, gentle, whimsical, uncluttered, premium but approachable, natural props and soft surfaces.

Off brand

Neon, harsh flash, distorted faces, extra limbs, generic bear replacements, fake logos, random text, watermarks, clutter, overly saturated or sinister moods.

Character accuracy

Rilakkuma: brown with cream muzzle and yellow ear interiors. Korilakkuma: cream-white with pink ear interiors. Kiiroitori: small yellow bird. Chairoikoguma: darker brown cub. Maintain canonical appearance using official reference images where permitted.

Production note

These are concept dimensions. Confirm printer-specific bleed, trim, safe areas and resolution before final production. For official product representations, use verified licensed product photos.
