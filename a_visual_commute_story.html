Behind The Transformation

Photoshop Generative Workflow

How AI generative tools turn mundane metropolitan transit routes into surreal cosmic journeys.

Generative Fill

Replaces ordinary concrete platforms and tracks with organic elements like tranquil water, koi fish, bioluminescent moss, and glowing runes.

Sky Replacement

Swaps standard grey urban skies with deep cosmic nebulae, oversized moons, and starry canopies that frame skyscraper silhouettes.

Lighting Filter

Blends ambient reflections on rain-slicked pavement and glass facades to match the soft glow of street lamps and celestial bodies.

Generative Expand

Extends original photo borders seamlessly to craft cinematic vertical aspect ratios ideal for storytelling visual galleries.
